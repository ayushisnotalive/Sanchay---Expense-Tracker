## Full Description (4000 characters max)

**Take complete control of your financial freedom with Sanchay.**

Sanchay is a modern, high-performance wealth and personal finance suite designed for individuals who demand precision, privacy, and seamless speed. Built with cutting-edge Android Jetpack Compose and an enterprise-grade Java 21 Spring Boot backend, Sanchay brings the power of institutional wealth management into your pocket.

### 🌟 WHY CHOOSE SANCHAY?

#### 📱 100% Offline-First Architecture
Your data belongs to you. Sanchay works flawlessly without internet access. Add expenses, manage multiple wallets, and track investments anytime, anywhere. When you connect online, Sanchay's bi-directional delta synchronization seamlessly and securely updates your cloud vault in milliseconds.

#### 🧠 Enterprise AI Financial Advisory
Get intelligent, context-aware financial advice without exposing your private keys or compromising your data. Sanchay analyzes your spending trends, discretionary budgets, and debt repayment trajectories to provide personalized recommendations for growing your net worth.

#### ⚡ Sub-Millisecond Speed & Zero Lag
Engineered with indexed Room database storage, Compose stable renders, and Java 21 Virtual Threads, Sanchay renders complex analytics and thousands of transactions with silky-smooth 60/120fps responsiveness.

#### 📊 14 Comprehensive Financial Hubs
- **Interactive Dashboard**: Real-time spending progress rings, cloud sync badges, and recent transactions.
- **Deep Analytics**: Category spending distributions, weekly and monthly trend breakdowns.
- **Multi-Wallet Support**: Seamlessly balance cash, bank accounts, and digital credit lines.
- **Subscription Monitor**: Track recurring subscriptions, renewal alerts, and monthly overheads.
- **Investment Portfolio**: Monitor stock, mutual fund, and crypto holdings alongside total net worth.
- **Debt & Loan Payoff**: Avalanche and snowball debt repayment plans with visual progress bars.
- **Savings Goals**: Set visual milestones for emergencies, vacations, and large purchases.
- **AI Advisor**: Interactive conversational finance intelligence.
- **Split Bills**: Group expense calculations with instant per-person settlement breakdowns.
- **Financial Calendar**: View expenses by date and audit your recurring commitments.
- **Real-Time Currency Converter**: Support for multi-currency transactions and global rates.
- **Smart Receipt Scanner**: On-device ML Kit OCR to instantly parse receipt totals and dates.

### 🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY FIRST
- **No Third-Party Trackers**: We do not sell your financial data to advertising networks.
- **Encrypted Synchronization**: Industry-standard JWT authentication and TLS 1.3 encryption.
- **Granular Permissions**: Camera is only requested when you scan receipts. Location is strictly optional for local spend tagging.
